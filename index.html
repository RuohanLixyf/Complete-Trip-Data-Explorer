About the dataset

Journeys, not isolated segments

The dataset represents complete multimodal journeys constructed from anonymized mobility observations. Each complete trip may include one or more travel segments and modes, allowing users to examine access, egress, transfers, and the main portion of a journey.

Study Area

The data cover the Salt Lake City metropolitan area and surrounding counties in Utah.
